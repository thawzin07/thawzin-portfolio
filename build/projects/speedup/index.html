    The Challenge

    Problem Statement

    Students often struggle to synthesize dense lecture slides and documents into effective study material, needing a rapid way to transform multi-format files into self-testing quizzes, flashcards, and personalized AI explanations without manual deck creation.

    💡
    Engineered Solution

    Key Innovation

    Adaptive learning engine parsing PDFs/DOCX with instant AI quizzes, flashcards & tutor chat.

    Engineering Ownership

    My Role & Key Contributions

    Role Overview: AI practice/tutor workflow, auth/storage integration, responsive UI, and deployment reliability contributor.
